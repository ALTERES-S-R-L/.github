## Our identity: the dynamic weight of excellence

*Ancient Greek · Halteres · The weights of flight*

In ancient Greece, **Halteres** (ἁλτῆρες) were stone or lead weights, sculpted to fit perfectly in the athlete's hands. During the long jump, athletes would swing the Halteres: the forward thrust at takeoff and the controlled release before landing manipulated the laws of physics to gain decisive centimeters.

For us, Artificial Intelligence and Big Data are the **Halteres** of the 21st century. Not tools that "run in place of the athlete," but **multipliers of effectiveness**. The scientific weight that, used with the right timing and technique, allows an organization to leap beyond the limits of traditional management.

> "Inspired by the ancient weights used to jump at the Olympics, Alteres provides the technological momentum needed to overcome limits. Just as athletes used the physics of the Halteres to fly further, we give organizations Big Data and AI tools to push performance and management beyond current standards."

## The Alteres method: *Il Doppio Salto*, the double jump

Our value lies in pairing solid engineering with strategic judgment. Two key phases transform the chaos of data into strategic direction.

**Phase 1. Data Architecture & Intelligence Discovery.** *"The Background Work: Research & Extraction."* Many sports organizations sit on gold mines of data buried in analogue formats or unstructured files. Our work begins where others stop.

- NLP and custom LLMs to read, interpret and catalogue thousands of documents (PDFs, race reports, historical databases).
- Custom data-gathering systems designed from scratch where data is missing or fragmented.
- Goal: transform "paper" into an "intelligent database": clean, queryable, and structurally solid.

**Phase 2. Strategic Synthesis & Decision Support.** *"The Technical Judgment: Interpretation & Vision."* Having data ready is only half the work. The real value of Alteres emerges here: in the ability to technically interpret those numbers for decision-makers.

- Beyond raw data: concrete solutions and actionable paths, not confusing dashboards.
- Domain consulting filtered through the lens of elite athletic experience: we distinguish "noise" from game-changing insight.
- Sensible use of AI: Generative or Agentic AI deployed only where it delivers real competitive advantage.

## Our work

### [TuttoAtletica](https://tuttoatletica.it)

*Digital Platform · NLP · Big Data*

A web portal dedicated to Italian track & field, built to make competitions, results, and athlete profiles instantly accessible to athletes, coaches, officials, and fans. Data is linked to official FIDAL sources, with thousands of unstructured race documents read and catalogued through custom NLP pipelines, then turned into a clean, queryable database.

- Advanced search across competitions by region, level, category, and discipline, with live countdowns and a full historical archive.
- Athlete profiles with complete participation history, podium highlights, and a follow system to track favourites.
- Race detail pages with disciplines, schedules, full results tables, and direct links to the official FIDAL page.
- Secure user accounts with email verification, encrypted credentials, and token-based authentication.

`React` · `Node.js API` · `MongoDB` · `NLP / LLM` · `FIDAL Data`
